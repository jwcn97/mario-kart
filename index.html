Kart Racer

Dev hub. The game runs on one shared screen; every player's phone is a tilt-to-steer wheel.

PHASE 3 · READY

Game screen

A 3D arena with one kart per player. Phones join by QR code; arrows and W A S D drive too.

 PHASE 3 · READY

Phone controller

Tilt to steer, thumbs on the gas and brake pads. Streams to the game screen 60 times a second.

Play with phones

  1. Open the game screen on this computer (or a TV or tablet).
  2. Put each phone on the same Wi-Fi and scan the QR code on the game screen.
  3. Accept the certificate warning once per phone (it's your own dev certificate).
